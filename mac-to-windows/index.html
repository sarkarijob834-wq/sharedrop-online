Send files from Mac to Windows

Move files between a Mac and a Windows PC with no cable and no cloud.

Steps

  1. Open the official ShareDrop.io website on your Mac and on your Windows PC.
  2. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi network. If they are on different networks, create a room on one device and open its link on the other.
  3. Wait until the other device appears on your screen as an icon.
  4. Click or tap that icon and choose the files you want to send.
  5. On the receiving device, accept the incoming file prompt.
  6. Keep both tabs open until the progress bar finishes, then save the file.

Why this works well

AirDrop does not reach Windows. A browser tool lets a Mac and a PC swap files on the same network in a few clicks.

Tips

  • Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox all work on the Mac.
  • Zip folders first so they travel as a single file.
  • Prefer 5 GHz Wi-Fi for faster local transfers.

Features can change over time. Check the official ShareDrop.io site for the latest details.
