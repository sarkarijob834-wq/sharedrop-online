Basics

How it works

Two browsers meet through a small helper service, then the file travels directly between them.

Same Wi-Fi

Open ShareDrop.io on two devices that share a network and they show up on each other's screen.

Rooms

A room link lets two devices find each other even when they are not on the same Wi-Fi.

Send text

Besides files, you can pass short text, such as a link or an address, to a connected device.

Compatibility

ShareDrop.io runs in modern browsers on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS.

Troubleshooting

Most problems come from the network, a VPN, or a tab that went to sleep.

Setup guide

You do not need an app or an account. Open the site and you are ready.

Is it safe?

Transfers use encrypted WebRTC connections. Your own habits matter just as much.

FAQ

Short answers to the questions people ask most.

Phone to PC

Move photos, 4K clips and documents to your computer without a cable or an app.

Computer to phone

Tablet to PC

Transfer notes, scans and photos from your tablet to a computer.

iPhone to Android

Mac to Windows

File types

Send photos

Share full-resolution photos between phones, tablets and computers.

Send videos

Multi-gigabyte videos can go straight across with no cloud upload.

Send PDF

Send PDFs, contracts and scans between phone and computer.

Send large files

Large files travel once, straight to the other device.

Alternatives

ShareDrop alternatives

If ShareDrop.io does not fit your setup, these are the options worth trying.

PairDrop

Both tools send files between browsers. Try each and keep the one that fits your devices.

Snapdrop

Same idea, different tools: local sharing in your browser.

AirDrop

AirDrop is great inside the Apple world. For everything else, use a browser.

WeTransfer

Cloud transfer sites make you upload first. A direct transfer skips that step.
